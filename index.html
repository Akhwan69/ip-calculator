Referensi cepat

      
Aturan perhitungan yang digunakan.

      

        

          /31
          Point-to-point (RFC 3021), 2 host, tanpa broadcast.
        

        

          /32
          1 host, tanpa broadcast, first = last = IP.
        

        

          /0–/30
          Hosts/Net = 2^(32-prefix) − 2.
        

        

          Validasi
          IP 4 oktet (0–255, tanpa leading zero), prefix 0–32.
